## Offside-restart hypothesis

Forest–Leeds contains **3 offsides per team**.

Opta's published event definitions establish two important semantics:

1. `Free kick taken` qualifier means **any free kick; direct or indirect**.
2. An `Offside` event is attributed when a **free kick is awarded** for offside.

Therefore a raw `Pass + FreekickTaken` population can include indirect free-kick restart passes generated by offsides, even if a provider's displayed 'Free-Kick passes' statistic excludes those offside restarts.

The arithmetic is exact in Forest–Leeds:

- Forest raw `FreekickTaken` passes 16 − 3 Leeds offsides = 13 displayed FK passes.
- Leeds raw `FreekickTaken` passes 16 − 3 Forest offsides = 13 displayed FK passes.

This is strong structural evidence, but it is **not yet sufficient to encode the rule**. We must prove event-level pairing rather than subtract headline offsides.
